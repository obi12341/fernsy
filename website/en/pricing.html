Pricing

Fernsy – free

Fernsy for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV costs nothing: live TV with a program guide, movies, series, Jellyfin and recordings on the Mac, without ads and without an account.

Fernsy Cloud – €3.49 per month

Price including VAT. The subscription renews every month and can be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of the paid month.

  • 100 GB of private storage for your recordings, end-to-end encrypted
  • Watch recordings on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, and download them on iPhone and iPad
  • The Fernsy assistant on all your devices, 300 requests per month
  • No account: your key is kept in your iCloud

How to subscribe

Fernsy from this website (Mac): Settings › Recordings › Fernsy Cloud › "Subscribe". You pay on fernsy.app through our reseller Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited), which handles payment, invoice and sales tax. Outside the euro area Paddle shows the price in your currency.

Fernsy from the App Store: as an in-app purchase through Apple. The App Store shows the price in your country before you buy.

Our terms of service and the refund and cancellation policy apply.
