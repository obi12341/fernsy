Your IPTV subscription as a real Mac app

Live TV with a full program guide, movies and series – fast, native and ad-free. For Xtream Codes accounts.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

For Macs with Apple silicon · macOS 27 or later

Live TV

  • Channel list with categories, search and favorites
  • Program guide grid for every channel
  • Switch channels with the arrow keys

Movies and series

  • Poster view, sorted by newest, name or rating
  • Seasons and episodes
  • Pick up right where you left off

Playback

  • Hardware decoding, up to 4K
  • GPU deinterlacing
  • Seamless reconnects when the network drops out

Privacy

Fernsy collects no data. Your credentials stay in your Mac’s keychain, and the app only connects to your provider.

Please note: Fernsy does not include any channels, movies or other content and does not sell subscriptions. You need an account (Xtream Codes API) with an IPTV provider of your choice.
