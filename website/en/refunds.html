Refunds and Cancellation

Last updated: November 2026. The German version is legally binding.

Purchases on fernsy.app

Fernsy Cloud on our website is sold by mobilistics GmbH; Stripe processes the payment. Refunds work as follows:

  • Withdrawal in the first 14 days: As a consumer you can withdraw from the contract within 14 days without giving a reason (see right of withdrawal). We then refund the first payment minus a proportionate amount for the days Fernsy Cloud was already running – at purchase you asked for it to start right away.
  • Renewals: We do not refund monthly renewals unless the law requires it. Cancel before the next renewal if you do not want to pay again.
  • Billing errors: We always refund duplicate charges and payments caused by a technical error.

Declare a withdrawal or ask for a refund by email to info@mobilistics.de, ideally from the address you paid with. The money goes back to the payment method you used.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time, without notice. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for; until then you can keep using Fernsy Cloud. Cancel without signing in on Cancel contracts here, in Fernsy on the Mac (Settings › Recordings › Fernsy Cloud › "Manage Subscription…") or by email. After the subscription ends, your recordings stay stored for 30 days and are then deleted.

Purchases in the App Store

Subscriptions you started in Fernsy from the App Store are sold by Apple. Apple alone decides on refunds; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Cancel in your Apple Account's subscription settings.

Your statutory rights

Statutory rights, such as the consumer right of withdrawal and warranty rights, remain unaffected.
