Refunds and Cancellation

Last updated: November 2026. The German version is legally binding.

Purchases on fernsy.app (through Paddle)

Fernsy Cloud on our website is sold by our reseller Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited). Refunds work as follows:

  • First payment: Within 14 days of a subscription's first payment you get it back in full on request, no reason needed. The subscription then ends at once.
  • Renewals: We do not refund monthly renewals unless the law requires it. Cancel before the next renewal if you do not want to pay again.
  • Billing errors: We always refund duplicate charges and payments caused by a technical error.

Ask for a refund through the link in Paddle's confirmation email, on paddle.net or by email to info@mobilistics.de. The money goes back to the payment method you used.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time, without notice. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for; until then you can keep using Fernsy Cloud. Cancel in Fernsy on the Mac (Settings › Recordings › Fernsy Cloud › "Manage Subscription…"), through the link in Paddle's email or on paddle.net. After the subscription ends, your recordings stay stored for 30 days and are then deleted.

Purchases in the App Store

Subscriptions you started in Fernsy from the App Store are sold by Apple. Apple alone decides on refunds; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Cancel in your Apple Account's subscription settings.

Your statutory rights

Statutory rights, such as the consumer right of withdrawal and warranty rights, remain unaffected. Paddle, as the seller, gives you the withdrawal information for purchases on fernsy.app with your purchase.
