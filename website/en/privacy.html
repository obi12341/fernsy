The Fernsy app

Fernsy contains no analytics, tracking or advertising services. Without Fernsy Cloud and the assistant, we receive no personal data from you.

The credentials you enter (server address, username, password) are stored in your device’s keychain. Fernsy connects only to your IPTV provider’s server to load channel lists, program data and streams. In doing so, your provider receives technically necessary data such as your IP address; your provider is responsible for this processing, and its privacy policy applies.

Favorites, playback positions, settings and cached catalog and program data are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

iCloud sync

When iCloud is on for your device, Fernsy syncs favorites, recently watched and hidden channels, settings and your accounts (including passwords) between your devices through your own iCloud. This data is stored in your iCloud account at Apple; we have no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Fernsy Cloud (subscription)

With Fernsy Cloud you can upload recordings from Fernsy on the Mac to private storage and watch them on your other devices. Recordings, titles, channels and program details are encrypted on your device before they leave it; the key is kept only in your iCloud. Neither we nor our storage provider Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) can read the content. Our server sees an account id derived from your key, the size and time of uploads, your IP address when you access it, and the status of your subscription, which we check with Apple or Stripe. The legal basis is the performance of the subscription contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). When your subscription ends, we delete your stored recordings after 30 days; you can delete all Fernsy Cloud data yourself at any time in Settings. You buy and manage the subscription through Apple in Fernsy from the App Store, and from us with payment through Stripe in Fernsy from this website (see "Purchase on fernsy.app").

Purchase on fernsy.app

We sell Fernsy Cloud on our website ourselves; Stripe processes the payment (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland). At purchase we pass your email address, country and postal code to Stripe to create the subscription and calculate VAT. You enter your payment details directly with Stripe; we do not see them. Stripe processes the data for payment, invoicing, tax and fraud prevention partly on our behalf and partly as an independent controller under Stripe's privacy policy. Our server stores only the customer and subscription ids and the subscription's status and term. In the Stripe dashboard we can also see the email address, country, postal code, invoices and payment history to handle requests, withdrawals and refunds; we keep invoice data for the periods required by commercial and tax law (up to ten years). The legal bases are the performance of the contract and our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). Where Stripe transfers data to the USA, this is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Stripe, Inc. is certified.

Assistant (part of Fernsy Cloud)

When you use the assistant, Fernsy sends your request and the conversation so far to our server and from there to OpenRouter (OpenRouter, Inc., USA), which passes it to the language model Claude by Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC, USA). So that the assistant can answer, a request contains the data it needs: channel names, program data, titles from your film, series and Jellyfin library, your favorites and recently watched items, and the time, time zone, language and device type.

Before the first request on each device, Fernsy asks for your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can withdraw it at any time in Settings under Fernsy Cloud. Our server does not store the content of your requests and answers and does not write it to logs; it stores only the account id, the time and the number of tokens processed, to count the monthly allowance, and deletes these entries after 40 days. OpenRouter’s and Anthropic’s privacy policies apply to their processing.

Where Fernsy Cloud and the assistant transfer data to the USA, the transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

When you download from the Mac App Store and for crash reports you have allowed in macOS, Apple processes data under Apple’s privacy policy; we only receive anonymized, aggregated information.
