## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Start on iPhone and iPad follows the clock
While Start is shown, Jetzt live SHALL show the show on now with its progress, moving on to the
next show within a minute of the change, and Heute Abend SHALL leave out shows that ended. The
guides of Start's channels SHALL be loaded again when they run short.

#### Scenario: iPad left on Start
- **WHEN** Start stays open on an iPad while a channel's show ends
- **THEN** within a minute its Jetzt live card shows the next show
